## How to verify search volume and competition
1. Open Google Keyword Planner through a Google Ads account.
2. Choose **Discover new keywords**.
3. Enter the keyword ideas or a relevant website.
4. Set the target location (for example, India) and language (English).
5. Review the available average monthly searches, competition, and bid estimates.
6. Record the date checked and the selected location.
7. Compare related terms and prioritize relevant phrases that match the actual services CodeAlpha offers.
8. If precise volume is unavailable, write **“not available”** instead of guessing.

### Suggested tracking sheet columns
Copy these columns into a spreadsheet when validating the keywords:

`Keyword | Location | Language | Avg. monthly searches | Competition | Search intent | Content page | Date checked | Tool/source`

## On-page SEO recommendations
- Use one clear primary keyword per page and related terms naturally.
- Write a unique title tag and meta description for each important page.
- Use descriptive page headings (H1, H2) and useful, original content.
- Link related blog posts and program pages internally.
- Make pages mobile-friendly and improve loading performance.
- Use descriptive image alt text.
- Keep program details, eligibility, dates, fees, certificates, and application steps accurate and transparent.
- Avoid keyword stuffing, copied content, or unsupported claims.

## Competitor analysis approach
Review relevant internship and learning platforms that appear for the target keywords. For each competitor, record:
- Page title and URL
- Main topic and search intent
- Helpful content or features
- Calls to action and application steps
- Gaps that CodeAlpha could address with clearer, more useful information

Do not assume a competitor is successful based only on its ranking. Confirm observations directly and record the date of review.
