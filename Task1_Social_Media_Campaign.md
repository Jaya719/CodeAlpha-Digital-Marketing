# Task 1: Social Media Campaign Design
## CodeAlpha — 7-Day Content Calendar

**Prepared by:** [Your Full Name]  
**Platforms:** LinkedIn and Instagram  
**Campaign objective:** Increase awareness, engagement, and trust among students interested in internships, coding, and technology careers.

### Target audience
College students, freshers, beginner programmers, and students looking for practical project experience.

### Content approach
- Keep posts useful, clear, and student-focused.
- Use a consistent visual style and CodeAlpha branding.
- Use a clear call to action (CTA) in every post.
- Only announce opportunities, dates, benefits, or claims that have been verified by CodeAlpha.
- Reply politely to genuine comments and questions.

## Seven-day calendar

| Day | Content theme | Format | Post concept | Call to action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Brand introduction | Static graphic / carousel | Introduce CodeAlpha and explain the value of learning through practical projects. | Follow the page for learning and internship updates. |
| Day 2 | Beginner learning tip | Carousel | Share 5 habits that help students learn coding consistently. | Save this post and share it with a classmate. |
| Day 3 | Project-based learning | Short reel / carousel | Show a simple workflow: identify a problem, plan a solution, build, test, and improve. | Comment with a project idea you want to build. |
| Day 4 | Career preparation | Static graphic | Share a student checklist: resume, GitHub profile, project explanation, and communication practice. | Save this checklist for your placement preparation. |
| Day 5 | Internship learning | Carousel | Explain how to make the most of an internship: set goals, document progress, ask questions, and meet deadlines. | Which skill are you working on this month? |
| Day 6 | Engagement prompt | Story / question post | Ask students which skill they want to improve: coding, design, marketing, or communication. | Vote or comment with your choice. |
| Day 7 | Weekly recap | Reel / carousel | Summarize the week’s learning tips and invite students to follow future updates. | Follow and share with a student who may find it useful. |

## Creative and design recommendations
- Use a consistent brand colour palette and readable fonts.
- Keep each graphic focused on one key message.
- Use high-contrast text and accessible font sizes.
- Add a clear CTA and the official CodeAlpha handle where appropriate.
- Use only approved logos and genuine project screenshots; do not imply endorsements or outcomes that have not been verified.

## Measurement plan
Track these metrics weekly:
- Reach and impressions
- Likes, comments, shares, and saves
- Follower growth
- Profile visits
- Link clicks, if a link is included

Calculate engagement rate consistently using the platform’s available data. For example, state whether the calculation uses reach or followers as the denominator; do not mix methods when comparing posts.

## Expected outcome
This calendar provides a structured starting point for regular posting, useful student content, and audience interaction. Actual performance should be evaluated after publishing using platform analytics.
